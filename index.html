About David

Experienced. Credentialed. Multilingual.

I am a certified ESL and Multilingual Literacy educator with an M.A. in TESOL from The New School and nearly two decades of experience teaching in K–12 classrooms in New Jersey and Brazil. I currently serve as a teacher of record at Bernards High School and co-teach across Biology, Chemistry, World History, and Geometry.

My approach combines high academic expectations with the warmth and scaffolding every learner needs to genuinely thrive — not just pass a test, but build lasting skills and lasting confidence.

I work with students at all levels, from foundational literacy to advanced writing, from basic arithmetic to Geometry and Algebra. Multilingual families are especially welcome — I conduct sessions in English, Portuguese, or Spanish as needed.

My Philosophy

How I Work with Students

High Standards, Real Scaffolding

I never lower expectations — I build the supports that allow students to meet them. Every student is capable of rigorous, meaningful work when the right structures are in place.

Meaning Over Memorization

I assess and reward understanding, effort, and growth — not just surface-level recall. Students who work with me learn how to think, not just what to say on a test.

Student-Centered Planning

Every student is different. I design sessions around your child's specific goals, learning profile, and academic needs — not a generic curriculum.
