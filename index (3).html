About

Twenty Years of
Transforming Learners

I'm David Venezia, a certified ESL and Multilingual Literacy educator currently teaching at Bernards High School in the Somerset Hills School District. I hold a Master of Arts in TESOL from The New School and bring two decades of classroom experience — including five years as AP Language teacher and AP Coordinator at Our Lady of Mercy School in Rio de Janeiro.

My approach combines high academic expectations with the flexibility to meet each student where they are. Whether a student is navigating English as a new language, struggling with reading fluency, or preparing for AP-level writing, I design instruction that builds genuine competence — not just short-term test performance.

My Philosophy

High Standards.
Human-Centered Instruction.

Diagnostic First

Every engagement starts with understanding the student — not just their gaps, but their strengths, their learning style, and what motivates them. Assessment is a conversation, not a verdict.

Scaffolded Toward Independence

I use structured supports to guide students through challenging material, then gradually remove those supports as competence grows. The goal is autonomous learners, not tutor dependency.

Cross-Curricular Literacy

Reading and writing are not just English skills — they're tools for thinking in every discipline. I bring content-area literacy into science, history, and math so students can access the full curriculum.

Rigorous & Warm

High expectations and genuine care are not opposites. I hold students to meaningful academic standards while ensuring every session feels safe, respectful, and encouraging.
